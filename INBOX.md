# INBOX — source material for EDA routine v3, modes A/B/C

Edited by the user. The routine reads this at MODE SELECTION (RUNBOOK_v3.md) and, for modes A/B/C, may take at most ONE
item (B) or ONE group (C) per session, or as many one-liners from §A as it chooses to open as separate hypothesis candidates
in Phase 1 (still capped at 6 candidates total there). After a session finishes Phase 3, it marks the item(s) it used —
never before, and never anything it didn't use. The routine may not add, remove or reword an entry itself; new
ideas it wants to propose go in that run's own `INBOX_PROPOSALS.md` for you to merge here by hand.

Format for the `used` mark, appended to the end of the line: `  [used: <run folder>]`. An item with no `[used: ...]` tag is
available. A used item is left in place, not deleted, so the record of what's been tried survives.
